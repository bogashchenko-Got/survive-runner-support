PeopleBase Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

PeopleBase is a business card scanning and contact management application. This Privacy Policy explains how PeopleBase handles information when you use the app.

1. Information processed by PeopleBase

PeopleBase may process information contained on business cards and QR codes, including names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, addresses, notes, and other contact information.

The app may also process images selected from your device or captured using your device camera in order to scan business cards and recognize text.

2. Local data storage

Contact information created or scanned in PeopleBase is stored locally on your device.

PeopleBase does not operate its own cloud server for storing your contact database.

3. Business card scanning and OCR

PeopleBase uses on-device text recognition and barcode/QR recognition technologies to extract information from business cards and QR codes.

Images and recognized contact information are processed for the purpose of providing the app's scanning and contact-management functions.

4. Camera and photo access

PeopleBase may request access to your camera or photo library when you choose to scan or import a business card.

This access is used only when required for features initiated by you.

5. Export, sharing, backup and transfer

PeopleBase allows you to export or share contact information using formats such as CSV, VCF, or backup files.

Information is shared only when you choose to use an export, backup, transfer, or sharing function.

When you share or export information, it may be handled by third-party apps, services, or storage locations selected by you. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Personal data collection

PeopleBase does not require you to create an account to use the main contact management features.

PeopleBase does not sell your personal information or your scanned contact information to advertisers or third parties.

7. Purchases

PeopleBase may offer optional paid plans or in-app purchases.

Payments are processed by the applicable app store, such as Google Play or Apple App Store. PeopleBase does not receive or store your full payment card information.

8. Data deletion

You can delete individual contacts or multiple contacts directly from the PeopleBase application.

You can remove locally stored PeopleBase data by deleting the data inside the app or uninstalling the application from your device.

9. Children

PeopleBase is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to design PeopleBase so that contact information is handled securely. However, no device, storage system, or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PeopleBase features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PeopleBase privacy practices, please contact the developer through the support contact listed on the PeopleBase Google Play or Apple App Store page.
